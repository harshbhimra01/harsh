Moved 40 services onto a shared Kubernetes platform

Cloud engineering, 2023 to 2024. Nine clusters, no shared anything — down to two.

EKSTerraformHelmIstioExternalDNS
9 → 2Clusters to patch
40Services, one platform
2wk → 1dService onboarding

01 · SITUATION

Every team ran its own kingdom.

Before
40 services across 9 EKS clusters. No shared ingress, no consistent autoscaling, and cloud spend growing faster than traffic. Every upgrade was nine upgrades.

02 · CONSTRAINT

Teams feared losing control.

Constraint
Nobody would move onto a platform that felt like a downgrade. Isolation, quotas and self-service onboarding were non-negotiable — the migration had to sell itself team by team.

03 · THE CALL

Multi-tenant, one front door.

Changed
A single multi-tenant EKS platform: namespaces per team, one Istio ingress, consistent Helm chart templates, and a Terraform module every team used to onboard a service.
Tradeoff
Kept two clusters instead of one for blast-radius separation. Slightly more to run, far easier for every team to say yes to.

04 · RECEIPT

Onboarding went from weeks to a day.

After
Two clusters instead of nine. New services onboard in under a day. Kubernetes-layer spend down roughly a third at flat traffic.9 → 2 clustersonboard 2wk → 1d
