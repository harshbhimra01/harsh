Selected work

Every write-up answers the same three questions: what was there before, what I changed, and what was different afterwards. Each panel takes the colour of the line the work belongs to.

Delivery

Cut deploy time from 40 minutes to 6

Platform team, 2024, 4 engineers

GitHub Actions, Argo CD, Helm, Terraform

Before: 40minAfter: 6min

Median deploy time

Before
Releases were run by hand from a wiki checklist. A typical deploy took 40 minutes, needed a senior engineer on standby, and failed about once in five attempts.
What I changed
Rebuilt the pipeline as a single GitHub Actions workflow: build, test, image push, then GitOps sync with Argo CD. Wrote the environment config in Terraform so staging and production share one module. Added a manual approval gate only for production.
After
Median deploy time 6 minutes. Deploy failures down to about 1 in 40. Any engineer on the team can now ship without the on-call lead.

Platform

Moved 40 services onto a shared Kubernetes platform

Cloud engineering, 2023 to 2024

EKS, Terraform, Helm, Istio, ExternalDNS

Before: 9After: 2

Kubernetes clusters to run and patch

Before
Each team ran its own EKS cluster. 40 services across 9 clusters, no shared ingress, no consistent autoscaling, and cloud spend growing faster than traffic.
What I changed
Designed a single multi-tenant EKS platform with namespaces per team, one Istio ingress, consistent Helm chart templates and a Terraform module every team used to onboard a new service.
After
Nine clusters down to two. New service onboarding went from two weeks to under a day. Cloud spend on the Kubernetes layer dropped by roughly a third at flat traffic.

Reliability

SLO-based alerting that cut pages by 70%

SRE, 2024

Prometheus, Grafana, Alertmanager, OpenTelemetry

Before: 15minAfter: 3min

Mean time to detect an incident

Before
Alerts fired on host metrics: CPU, disk, memory. On-call was paged dozens of times a week, and most pages were noise nobody acted on. Real incidents were often found by users first.
What I changed
Defined SLIs and SLOs for the top user journeys, moved alerting to burn-rate alerts on those SLOs, added tracing with OpenTelemetry, and rewrote the runbooks so each alert links to one.
After
Pages down by about 70%. Mean time to detect fell from about 15 minutes to under 3. The team now trusts the pager again.
