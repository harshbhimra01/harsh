About

I work on the parts of software that users never see until they break: cloud accounts, clusters, pipelines and dashboards. I want shipping a change to be routine, and I want to hear about a failure from a graph before I hear about it from a user.

Day to day that means AWS and Azure, Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD, GitOps and observability, with reliability targets and security checks built into the pipeline. I also work on the MLOps, LLMOps and AIOps side, where models are treated as services: versioned, deployed automatically and monitored.
