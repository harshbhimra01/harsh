SLO-based alerting that cut pages by 70%

SRE, 2024. The pager went from noise to signal.

01 · SITUATION

The pager cried wolf nightly.

Before
Alerts fired on host metrics: CPU, disk, memory. Dozens of pages a week, most of them noise nobody acted on. Real incidents were usually found by users first — the worst possible monitoring outcome.

02 · CONSTRAINT

Nobody trusted alerts anymore.

Constraint
Deleting alerts outright would blind us; keeping them kept everyone numb. The fix had to page less while catching more — credibility first, coverage second.

03 · THE CALL

Page on journeys, not hosts.

Changed
Defined SLIs and SLOs for the top user journeys, moved alerting to burn-rate alerts on those SLOs, added tracing with OpenTelemetry, and rewrote the runbooks so each alert links to exactly one.
Tradeoff
Host metrics stayed — as dashboards, not pages. Disk-full still gets caught, but by a ticket on a Tuesday, not a 3am page.

04 · RECEIPT

The team trusts the pager again.

After
Pages down about 70%. Mean time to detect from ~15 minutes to under 3. Try the alert yourself in the lab.pages −70%MTTD 15 → 3 min
