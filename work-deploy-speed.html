Cut deploy time from 40 minutes to 6

Platform team, 2024, 4 engineers. One pipeline instead of a wiki checklist.

GitHub ActionsArgo CDHelmTerraform
40→6Min median deploy
1/5 → 1/40Deploy failure rate
1Workflow, end to end
0Seniors needed on standby

01 · SITUATION

Releases ran on a wiki checklist.

Before
A typical deploy took 40 minutes, needed a senior engineer on standby, and failed about once in five attempts. Knowledge lived in one person's head and a wiki page nobody trusted.

02 · CONSTRAINT

Staging and prod had drifted apart.

Constraint
Two environments, two sets of config, no shared module. Any pipeline I built had to reunite them first, or automation would just ship drift faster.

03 · THE CALL

One workflow, GitOps sync, one gate.

Changed
A single GitHub Actions workflow: build, test, image push, then GitOps sync with Argo CD. Environment config in Terraform with staging and production sharing one module. A manual approval gate only for production.
Tradeoff
Kept the human gate for prod instead of full auto-deploy. Slower than pure CD by minutes, safer by trust — the team adopted it in a week because nothing scary changed on day one.

04 · RECEIPT

Any engineer can ship now.

After
Median deploy 6 minutes, failures about 1 in 40, no on-call lead required. The checklist wiki now redirects to the pipeline run page.40 → 6 minfail 1/5 → 1/40
